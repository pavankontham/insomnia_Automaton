# Site templates / demo craft

Demos are the product. Weak HTML does not get pitched.

## Standard
- Use FreeLLMAPI `auto:smart` (`task: code`) to generate a full single-file site.
- Fallback: niche craftsmanship template in `src/business/sites.ts` (still premium).
- Facts only: never invent prices, awards, street addresses, or review counts.
- Brand-first full-bleed hero, expressive fonts (no Inter/Roboto/Arial), atmosphere, 2–3 motions.
- QA gate before outreach: score ≥ **85**. Rebuild until ready.

## Pitch rule
Only contact buyers after the demo looks like something they would pay for.
When they like the demo, go 100% on the paid original — then invoice (Stripe when ready).
